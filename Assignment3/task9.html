Top 3 Travel Destinations

1. Paris, France

Paris is famous for its art, culture, and the Eiffel Tower.

“Paris is not a city; it’s a world.”

2. Tokyo, Japan

Tokyo blends tradition and futuristic innovation beautifully.

“A city that never stops surprising you.”

3. New York, USA

The city that never sleeps — a global hub of creativity and ambition.

“New York is not a place; it’s a dream.”

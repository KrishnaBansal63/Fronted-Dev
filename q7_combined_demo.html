Understanding Responsive Design

Responsive web design is an approach that makes web pages render well on a variety of devices and window or screen sizes. Some key concepts include:

  • Fluid grids using relative units
  • Flexible images that scale within their containers
  • Media queries to adapt layouts to different viewports
  • Mobile-first approach to design

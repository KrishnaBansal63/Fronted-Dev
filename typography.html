Typography and Text Styling Showcase

Typography plays a vital role in creating a pleasant reading experience. It defines how text looks and feels on a webpage. Choosing the right combination of font families, sizes, and spacing can significantly improve readability and design harmony. Designers often use sans-serif fonts for digital displays because they are cleaner and easier to read. Meanwhile, serif fonts are popular in printed materials as they add a touch of elegance and tradition. A good typographic layout maintains balance between contrast, alignment, and spacing. Elements like line-height and letter-spacing help in improving the overall flow of the text, ensuring that readers can enjoy content comfortably for longer periods.
